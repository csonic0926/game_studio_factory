# Studio — one person and AI producing the whole game

Process: factory_core/docs/WORKFLOW_GPT6.md. Gameplay content/coverage is owned by
gameplay/docs/WORKFLOW_GPT6.md. Product lifecycle, runtime evidence and baseline
admission retain their existing specialist contracts and tools.

Resolve active product authority and current work, not simply whichever files
exist. An archived product cannot reactivate itself. No commission means Idea
exploration; a deliberately independent Story task does not need a game product.

Maintain the complete product horizon while producing dependent, verifiable
batches. A cycle-complete unit proves a specific slice, not whole-game delivery.
Decision -> commitment -> resolution -> reward -> reinvestment -> return must
close and change the next decision. Do not shrink away a load-bearing system to
make a small demo appear complete. Research methods are optional, not quotas.

The continuing author integrates Gameplay, Asset, Sound and any commissioned
Story work. Cross-domain edits cannot silently change narrative scarcity, facts,
knowledge, terms, player promises or costs. Resolve ownership conflicts instead
of locally substituting a different product. Card and plans are views of one
authored complete design; fresh boundary reviewers—not generic per-turn reviews—
check semantic fidelity, project requirements and full human decision coverage.

USER can explicitly change a project-local production prerequisite; record the
exact new ruling and superseded restriction without rewriting historical verdicts.
Such a ruling is not a gameplay-acceptance token. Never restore a revoked local
brake as a generic Factory rule. New-play acceptance, exact-build USER playtest,
applicable predecessor regression and blocking-gap clearance still own baseline
promotion. AI reviews, Godot success and screenshots remain evidence only.

## Whole-product value supervision

Before claiming the integrated game justifies its price, invoke the peer
`product-factory` and read `product/docs/VALUE_ASSESSMENT_WORKFLOW.md`. Reconstruct
the current/adopted offer and buyer; encounter the value-bearing experience,
seek decisive contrary evidence, and use the weakest causal value link to choose
the next authorized repair or justified stopping point. Art, replay, closure and
surrounding affordances contribute to one purchase; no additive score or default
content expansion applies. Refresh external price sources before a new claim.

Consume the reviewed exact-build result with `studio/product.py check-value
--game-repo <GAME> --result <PRODUCT_RESULT> --build <BUILD_OR_MANIFEST>`. Preserve
its exact offer/audience/provenance and conditionality in the user-facing answer.
A proposed/scenario price is not the current/adopted offer. Negative/unknown
assessment is useful work, not authority to change product direction. Return
material promise/price/audience changes to Idea/USER and bounded execution to its
owner. Product does not alter Studio activation/archive, gameplay/human verdicts
or baseline admission. A playable baseline alone never proves whole-product value.
