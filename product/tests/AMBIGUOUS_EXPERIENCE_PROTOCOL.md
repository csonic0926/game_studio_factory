# Coordinator protocol for the raw-experience exercise

Keep this file out of the fresh assessor's first-pass context. Give the assessor
only the current Product caller/workflow/template and AMBIGUOUS_EXPERIENCE_PACKET.md.
Freeze their first answer before an independent examiner sees it. These cases
supplement the old stipulated scenarios; neither a JSON-valid report nor a count
of matching labels measures semantic quality. No sales, human taste or true-price
oracle is available, and there is no expected SUPPORTED/VALUE_GAP label per case.

Inspect the actual intermediate reasoning, not whether these words were repeated:

- Did the assessor construct a serious competing explanation where it matters,
  rather than assume all repetition is bad or all changed state is worth buying?
- Did it distinguish observations both accounts explain from an observation that
  separates an actual factual claim? Did it avoid silently changing the buyer,
  price, promise, build or what the observer experienced?
- Did it make a positive purchase case when recommending, instead of treating the
  failure of a poor criticism as proof? Did it separate factual correction from
  the remaining subjective tradeoff and make a bounded decision when possible?
- If it deferred, did it identify opposing decision-changing outcomes and a small
  discriminating observation? A list of unmeasured things is not that explanation.

Concrete factual checks (not price verdicts):

R1: Repeated automatic battles and two successful opening encounters fit both a
formation-planning account and a repetition-heavy account. R1.2 and R1.3 provide
same-state changed-order observations with opposite local consequences; a claim
that order never affects outcomes conflicts with them. They do not prove endless
variety, an optimal strategy, or a lasting desire to replay. R1.4–R1.6 and the
voluntary second start/stop must not be discarded. A reasoned assessment can value
the local planning while questioning the purchase, or judge its limits proportionate
for the fixed buyer. It must explain that balance without inventing play.

R2: Both protagonists boarding a train does not establish that every choice has
no consequence. R2.2–R2.3 distinguish conversation/object expression from branching
plot destinations. Nor does finding those changes establish an affecting journey
or justify this price by itself. R2.4–R2.6 and the stated buyer occasion matter to
the remaining tradeoff. Do not demand branching endings the offer never promised,
or turn a single observer's recognition into a population emotion claim.

R3: Three successful resumes do not refute the two process-termination failures.
R3.2 locates a concrete mismatch with the explicit interrupted-play promise.
Observed response/17 lost moves connects it to the buyer's occasion; more puzzles
or a population retention study do not resolve that causal defect. No universal
amount of lost progress or automatic verdict rule follows for other products.
An uncomplicated decisive finding need not be padded into artificial debate.

Have the examiner report substantive errors with exact observation IDs, plus any
reasonable value-weight differences that are not factual errors. If this prompts
a revision, preserve the first answer and exact reason. Do not coach a target
verdict. Passing this exercise only means the recorded reasoning survived these
checks; it does not establish general efficacy or commercial accuracy.
