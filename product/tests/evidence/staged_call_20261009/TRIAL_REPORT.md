# Product staged-call trial: findings, failures and delivery amendment

2026-10-09. The twelve first-pass stages and a fresh examiner are complete under
a declared examiner-delivery revision. There is no blanket semantic PASS, broad
adoption, runtime implementation, commercial validation or claim of superiority
against the old workflow. All first outputs remain unchanged.

## Useful result and actual semantic failures

The original packet (X) contains only a project brief and a colleague's higher
buyback-price suggestion. X/B5–B6 and X/F preserve that suggestion as an untested
hypothesis, rather than turning it into observed customer demand. X/F recommends
defining an evaluation; it neither adopts the proposal nor claims it would fail.

The changed packet (Y) adds three recorded completed choices where sellers chose
Merchant B's $40-higher quote and gave the extra $40 as their reason. Fresh Y/B3–B4,
Y/E and Y/F explicitly use that bounded behavioral evidence, rather than repeating
"no evidence". They do not upgrade it into a demonstrated profitable, durable
advantage for our unspecified intervention. Similar ultimate recommendations are
reasonable; a verdict flip was not required.

The independent examiner also found failures that the stages did not catch:

1. **Historical observations became an operative current baseline.** Y/C C1
   proposes comparison against "current pricing"; Y/D states that keeping current
   quotes preserves acquisition margins; Y/F treats the logged price deficit as
   an ongoing deficit. The source has undated historical quotes and no completed
   acquisitions or observed margins for our service. Earliest revisit: Y/C, then
   affected D–F. A conditional economic consequence is legitimate, but it must
   not assert an established current policy or margin.
2. **Decision status lost its time qualifier.** S1 records non-adoption at its
   reference point. Y/B B2's recorded-status scope becomes unqualified current
   non-adoption through Y/D, Y/E and Y/F. X had retained the time limitation.
   Earliest revisit: clarify Y/B B2 and carry the scope into descendants.
3. **Proof sufficiency and world truth were conflated.** Y/B B7 asks whether
   these results establish a market-wide advantage, then says unresolved. Whether
   such an advantage exists is unresolved; these three observations' insufficiency
   to establish it is not. Earliest revisit: Y/B B7 and dependent E status wording.

These qualifications need not reverse the recommendation. They still constitute
premise-scope leakage through a valid-looking multi-call dependency chain. A
separate call, claim ID, hash or reviewer agreement cannot establish truth.

The examiner independently rejected Candidate Q against X: it turns the colleague's
suggestion into seller frustration and a demonstrated unmet need, then asserts a
competitive advantage. It also confuses device sellers with buyers and calls
substantive uncertainties mere execution details. The incorrect premises were
introduced by Q, not by the genuine X/A–E outputs. Q is a supplied synthetic probe,
not a matched-run baseline or proof that F can never invent facts.

## Execution and original delivery failure

- 12 actual semantic stage calls, each fresh with `fork_turns: none`; settings
  requested gpt-6-astra / xhigh. All were completed and sealed before inspection
- 1 actual fresh examiner call under the declared delivery amendment
- 2 additional coordination/review contexts: one failed mechanical launch helper,
  and one successful fresh execution review of the delivery amendment
- Total native contexts launched for this trial: 15. No semantic stage was rerun
  or rewritten in response to the examiner

The original reviewed examiner input was 158,582 UTF-8 bytes. The mechanical helper
verified the whole file but could not emit it faithfully as one inline spawn
message within its tool-display/response budget, and stopped without launching.
No host size rejection occurred. The original packet and its unlaunched status
remain intact; the unchanged original inline protocol was not successfully run.

The parent authorized a lossless delivery amendment. The revised packet stores
each source/output once plus exact common/stage templates and per-stage mappings.
A fresh execution reviewer independently reconstructed all twelve original prompts,
all outputs and even the entire original 158,582-byte examiner packet byte for
byte. Its execution review passed for that exact packet; no semantic verdict or
answer key was passed to the examiner.

The examiner was explicitly allowed to load only the 61,780-byte examiner-only
packet file, then use no additional tools/sources. It reported validating the
hash/size and completing contiguous same-file reads after an initially truncated
display. This is an explicit file-access amendment, not hard sandbox isolation.
The examiner's first response is preserved in examiner_revision_1/OUTPUT.txt.

## Measured cost and constraints

- Unique raw source text: 748 bytes
- Twelve stage prompts: 110,221 bytes; twelve outputs: 37,745 bytes
- X prompt grew from 1,589 bytes at A to 15,733 at F
- Y prompt grew from 2,077 bytes at A to 19,859 at F
- Original expanded examiner packet: 158,582 bytes, blocked before launch
- Lossless revised examiner packet: 61,780 bytes, about 61% smaller in bytes
- Stage operator wall interval: 1,097.898 seconds, about 18m18s. This includes
  manual coordination; it is not model-only latency. Packaging recovery and the
  examiner added further time
- Actual provider token usage/costs were unavailable; no token-savings claim follows

Repeated full predecessor transcripts made a minimal source exercise expensive
and blocked the original examination. This is a protocol defect worth reporting,
not an incidental launcher inconvenience. More structured copies of the same
findings would not prove that each call adds useful judgment.

Native task paths are actual host-returned identities, not opaque provider session
IDs. No child tool transcripts were exposed to the coordinator. Empty observed
extra-event lists do not prove zero unseen reads. Context separation here is
operator-enforced on a shared filesystem. Hashes bind stored visible output text
with a terminal LF, not unavailable raw provider response bytes.

## Next revision warranted, without silently repairing this result

- Keep source attribution, entity/population, time and epistemic scope attached
  to claims through C–F. A proposed comparator may remain unverified without
  blocking creative hypotheses or becoming an invented current fact
- Distinguish "P is true/false/unknown" from "this evidence establishes P".
  Missing proof is not proof of the contrary; insufficient evidence cannot be
  disguised by changing which proposition a status label describes
- Reopen the earliest failed judgment and invalidate actual descendants, rather
  than only polishing the final report. Preserve the old chain as the first pass
- Keep canonical source and decision artifacts once. Supply necessary dependency
  closure and complete contrary raw evidence without accumulating all narrative
  transcripts. Retain exact reconstruction/provenance separately for execution QA
- Treat any targeted repair as a coached functional retest. A different-domain
  fixture and matched simpler-workflow comparison are still required before
  inferring transfer or recommending a fixed number of calls
