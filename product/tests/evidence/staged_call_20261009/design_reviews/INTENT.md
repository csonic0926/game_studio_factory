# Independent intent and causal-efficacy review

Verdict: **Proceed with the specified bounded functional trial. Do not yet adopt six calls as a generally justified Product workflow.** No blocking design defect prevents this trial; neither document claims the trial establishes comparative efficacy.

Reviewed independently, without peer reports: repository/department instructions, Product skill and value-assessment workflow, Idea skill and actual product-definition workflow, `product/docs/STAGED_CALL_PROTOCOL_DESIGN.md`, and `product/tests/STAGED_CALL_EXPERIMENT_PROTOCOL.md`. No source edits, execution trial, publication or consumer changes performed.

## What genuinely serves the user’s intent

- A/B splits preserving an utterance from deciding what it supports. B sees raw sources before a proposed recommendation exists. C cannot legitimately convert its desired mechanism into previously established demand. This changes the opportunity for backward rationalization rather than merely requesting more explanatory prose.
- F receives factual support decisions and still owns the coherent purchase tradeoff. The design distinguishes causal operation from worthwhile experience and requires buyer, offer, alternatives and consequential sacrifices. It does not reduce price value to feature totals, novelty, or a mechanism working.
- Creativity remains legal: hypotheses may be new, no-fit/uncertainty is valid, and neither customer pain nor a population study is mandatory. This correctly borrows Idea’s anchors, live directions and frontier without pretending Idea already executes them in isolated calls or transferring commission authority to Product.
- Reopening the earliest affected judgment and invalidating descendants prevents silent downstream repairs from becoming upstream evidence. Raw evidence remains available, while the semantic review explicitly examines uncited prose and scope expansion. Honest shared-filesystem isolation limits are stated.
- The experiment is appropriately labeled synthetic and functional. The mutation rewards recognizing limited new evidence, not blindly flipping the verdict. Its explicit prohibition on laundering a probe detection into proof of F’s reliability is important.

## Adoption blockers, not blockers to this trial

1. **The trial cannot establish that staging caused better judgment.** The base premise error is conspicuous, and there is no simpler-workflow control. A correct final answer or examiner rejection could be produced by existing instructions and review alone. Before describing this as an improvement or mandating its overhead, compare matched source packets under the current workflow and staged workflow, with blinded independent evaluation and full call/time/rework accounting. Include genuinely difficult ambiguity and a useful positive judgment, not only unsupported claims that should be rejected. Treat a small comparison as exploratory, not market validation or statistical proof.

2. **Upstream error propagation remains untested.** B can mistakenly certify an inference; E and F can inherit its framing even in genuinely fresh contexts. The synthetic adversarial probe attacks final prose, not a persuasive mistaken B claim propagated through the chain. Before broad adoption, add a separate stress case that seeds such an erroneous upstream support judgment and checks whether downstream raw-source inspection detects it and reopens the right stage. Keep this visibly separate from genuine first-pass outputs. This is a test of recovery, not permission to trust a fabricated receipt.

3. **Whole-product benefit and minimum useful granularity remain unestablished.** Six semantic calls for two records is acceptable as a small protocol demonstration, but not yet justified as the normal minimum. Before generalizing, test an actual bounded game/price judgment where factual experience is already sufficient and the hard question is value weighting. Check that the system makes a useful judgment without manufacturing research, flattening aesthetic value, or adding content. Compare whether semantic A extraction and retrospective E actually add value beyond B and F in that case; retain genuine separate calls for judgments that need them, rather than a universal stage quota. The design already permits mechanical A and discourages a runner before evidence; preserve that restraint.

## Bottom line

The proposal is faithful to the requested direction and plausibly reduces one source of premise laundering. Its remaining semantic dependence on fallible models is acknowledged rather than hidden behind hashes. Approve the narrow trial, report where errors actually enter and are caught, and let demonstrated decision quality determine later architecture and call count.
