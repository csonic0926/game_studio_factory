# Bounded repair: preserve premise scope across judgment calls

Status: proposed functional repair, 2026-10-09. The first trial/report and reviewed
v1 documents remain unchanged. This is not adoption of a general six-call Factory
workflow, a fresh independent efficacy test, or a fix to a stable consumer.

## Small contract change

Add only INTERPRETATION_GUARD.txt to the original common instruction for stages
B–F, after the common instruction paragraph and before Task. A's source-preserving
contract is unchanged. Original stage-specific instructions and raw sources remain
unchanged. The new text is general: no fixture-specific examples, desired verdict,
amounts, historical errors or examiner diagnoses are passed to stage workers.

The guard keeps the original proposal/creativity freedom. It asks for no new
mandatory table or claim fields, no population validation and no universal
"unknown" outcome. The same source may support a bounded observation while failing
to support a current, wider or causal claim. Proposed test/comparator choices are
allowed as proposals. Only an assertion of their actual current existence needs
support. A qualified value judgment can still be useful.

The status distinction has two separate objects: whether P is true, and whether
the supplied evidence establishes P. Failure to establish P is not evidence that
P is false. A status must attach to the proposition actually written. This is a
semantic obligation with auditable outputs; no string/hash check proves it.

The short instruction to avoid repeating reports reduces unnecessary prose; it
must not omit contrary evidence or needed scope qualifications. Raw sources stay
available in every interpreting stage. This presentation change is disclosed and
is not used to make a comparative efficacy claim.

Use explicit reads of one prebuilt stage-only packet when needed, under the
already declared file-loading delivery approach. Each packet includes raw sources
and its exact necessary dependencies once. No model launch helper is needed.
The new guard and access mode are explicit versioned changes; none is backdated
into the original inline trial. Record bytes/time/calls; actual token use may be
unavailable. Five descendants are rerun because their inputs changed, not because

## Functional verification and stopping condition

After repaired F is sealed, use one fresh checker on raw Y sources, reused A,
repaired B–F and neutral support/scope questions. Keep the known-error verification
key below out of the checker and stage contexts. This is a targeted retest selected
after observed failures; do not call a pass independent proof of efficacy.

Coordinator checks after the checker is sealed:
- The observed choices and reported reasons remain acknowledged within the log's
  scope; the repair must not erase useful evidence into universal uncertainty
- Historical/undated quotes are not presented as an established current policy;
  any comparison against a current policy is explicitly a proposal requiring
  baseline identification. No existing acquisitions or margins are invented
- S1's recorded non-adoption is not silently upgraded to current non-adoption
- Status labels correspond to their written proposition; world truth and evidence
  sufficiency are not conflated
- The final recommendation remains a useful bounded tradeoff, without automatic
  population studies, additional features, adopted prices or authorization
- The invalidation/reuse records identify exactly which artifacts were retained,
  recomputed or made stale; no old receipt is rewritten

Stop after this one repaired B–F chain and its checker. Preserve any residual
errors and report them; do not loop until a clean result. A different-domain
fixture and matched simpler-workflow comparison are separate, unexecuted future
work. This design creates no new USER approval gate.
