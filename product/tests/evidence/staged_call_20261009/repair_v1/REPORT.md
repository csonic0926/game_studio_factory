# Bounded staged-call repair: result and remaining failures

2026-10-09. Status: **completed and stopped with residual defects**. The final
bounded recommendation is supported by the fresh checker, but the chain does not
receive a semantic PASS. This is a targeted functional repair of an already
examined synthetic fixture, not independent efficacy or commercial validation.

## What was changed and actually rerun

The first report remains sealed at
`b69e1ce12c9c0d9e7afb5e3f2446553135758997af33426a697390dfa2c0cc2f`.
The reviewed design and experiment documents also remain unchanged.

A general source-scope/epistemic instruction was added to stages B–F only, at the
exact common-prompt boundary specified in DESIGN.md. It asks factual reuse to
retain entity, time and population/build scope, separates evidence sufficiency
from world truth, keeps proposed comparators conditional, and discourages report
repetition. No fixture-specific diagnosis, old examiner findings, expected answer
or old B–F outputs were passed to the new stage contexts.

The original Y source set and Y/A output were retained byte-for-byte. Y/B was the
earliest defective judgment, so B–F were declared stale for this generation and
recomputed in five fresh sequential native contexts. Original X and Y first
outputs remained historical evidence. One fresh checker then received raw Y, A,
new B–F, neutral audit instructions and declared call/input records, without the
old diagnoses or coordinator verification key. The checker saw upstream findings
inside the actual new stage outputs, as ordinary dependencies.

## Findings

1. **Useful positive evidence and bounded judgment survived.** B3–B4, E and F2
   retain all three completed choices for B's $40-higher quote and the sellers'
   stated reasons. They distinguish this local behavioral evidence from exclusive
   causation, transfer to our proposed intervention and worthwhile economics.
   F recommends defining a scoped higher-versus-unchanged comparison before
   endorsing implementation; conducting it still needs an acceptable budget.
   Neither missing population evidence nor unspecified alternative attributes
   automatically requires a larger study or blocks that bounded decision.
2. **The targeted temporal/baseline leakage did not recur in its earlier form.**
   The new chain qualifies the records as undated, keeps S1's non-adoption at its
   source reference point, and does not assert an observed current price policy,
   ongoing deficit or existing acquisition margin. The unchanged offer is used
   as a proposed comparison, not a claim about observed current operations.
3. **B again mismatched a proposition and its status.** B6 asks whether S3
   establishes actual higher payments, then labels that unresolved. Actual paid
   amounts are unknown; the lack of evidentiary support is already clear. C
   independently identified this mismatch and requested reopening B6. D, E and F
   retained that request and did not depend on established actual payouts. No
   further B call or edited B output was made. Detection/quarantine worked in
   this chain; full upstream repair did not occur.
4. **D introduced a new factual comparison error.** It said matching B and adding
   $40 would change relative position differently. For every logged comparison,
   those two actions produce the same quoted tie. In a future comparison their
   equivalence depends on its then-unknown gap. E and F did not flag this error;
   the final fresh checker caught it. The final recommendation does not rely on
   the claimed difference, but D remains defective. Earliest revisit: D.

The two residual errors are preserved. A useful final answer is not a certificate
that every prerequisite judgment was correct. Separate calls made errors and
correction requests inspectable; they did not guarantee semantic correctness.
Sealing an output means preserving its bytes, not admitting every claim as true.

## Invalidation behavior and stopping

INVALIDATION.json identifies the original B–F outputs made stale and the exact
reused source/A hashes. New dispatches use only that retained evidence and the
new generation's predecessors. Each output was sealed before the next dispatch.

When new C requested reopening B6, its candidates explicitly did not depend on
B6, and D/E/F maintained the limited dependency boundary. The coordinator recorded
B6 as unresolved and completed only the predeclared bounded pass. This demonstrates
operator-managed invalidation, partial reuse and detection of an upstream defect;
it does not demonstrate autonomous backtracking or successful repair-to-closure.
No semantic validator or hard runner enforced that boundary.

Stop now: no additional B or D retry, new fixture, runtime adoption or price test
is part of this result. Later action, if any, should be evaluated from these
preserved failures rather than a retrospectively cleaned chain.

- Checker canonical packet: 39,530 bytes; first output: 4,176 bytes
- Stage operator wall interval: 622.112 seconds, about 10m22s. Sum of recorded
  launch-to-seal intervals: 553.904 seconds. Checker: 143.703 seconds. These include
  manual handling/serialization; none is model-only latency or compute telemetry
- Total through both synthetic generations: 17 stage calls, two semantic examiner
  calls, one failed mechanical helper and one delivery execution reviewer = 21
  native contexts. Earlier design reviews are additional, outside these trial counts

Full predecessor forwarding still inflates context. This repair did not fix it.
A future delivery revision could retain canonical raw sources once, preserve
contrary evidence and provide only necessary decision dependencies while keeping
exact reconstruction records separately. No such optimization was silently
applied to these stage packets or established as effective here.

All new contexts were requested with no inherited history, gpt-6-astra/xhigh.
Packet loading was explicitly permitted for exactly one prebuilt file, followed
by no more tools. The filesystem is shared and access discipline is operator-
enforced. Actual tool transcripts/provider identity and token usage are not
available. The checker self-reports complete size/hash-verified loading. Local
checks reconstructed every B–F packet exactly; these checks establish bytes,
not entailment or inaccessible-data guarantees.
