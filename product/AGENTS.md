# Product Factory ownership

This peer department owns whole-product value judgment guidance, evidence/claim
contracts and their validators. Filled intent and assessments belong in the
explicit game repo under design/product_value, never in this checkout.

At game initialization, establish nonbinding whole-value intent with Idea via
setup.py init; read product/docs/PROJECT_INITIALIZATION_WORKFLOW.md. An unbuilt
game needs hypotheses and unknowns, never invented play or a commercial PASS.

Read product/skills/product-factory/SKILL.md and
product/docs/VALUE_ASSESSMENT_WORKFLOW.md. Idea retains commission; Studio's
studio/product.py retains activation/archive. Do not manufacture USER acceptance,
change price/audience/promise, migrate a game, or relink a consumer to pass a gate.
A valid negative/unknown assessment is useful completed work. No program proves
fun, aesthetics, buyer willingness or commercial success.

Standalone assessment is available without migrating historical games. Explicit
v2/v3 material design uses factory_core's selected process and two independent
complete-design reviews; a Product receipt cannot bypass that process.
