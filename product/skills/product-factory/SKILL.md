Read product/AGENTS.md, product/docs/PROJECT_INITIALIZATION_WORKFLOW.md and

inspect/context --capability product with the actual task (assessment for an

historical assessments need no migration, relink or new commission. Missing intent
does not invalidate an otherwise valid legacy assessment.

## Early intent and bounded learning

For new-game initialization use setup.py init; for additive intent in an existing
Idea-only game use product/product.py start --game-repo <GAME>. These preserve
existing authored work. Continue beyond seed creation:

1. Read the exact Idea sources and live lifecycle surfaced by inspection. Separate
   canonical authority from nonbinding exploration and Product intent. A partial
   or archived direction is not currently active merely because files exist.
2. Author design/product_value/PRODUCT_VALUE_INTENT.json with a provisional buying
   reason, adequacy case, identified value hypotheses, material unknowns and the
   smallest useful next test. Explain why the coherent whole could matter to its
   buyer, what observed benefit would be enough and what would defeat the case.
   Art, replay and supporting functions matter through that promise, not a quota.
3. Preserve honest UNKNOWN/null context or use an explicit conditional offer/buyer
   scenario within delegated scope. Every value_hypotheses entry remains a
   hypothesis. OPEN or DRAFTED is not approval. Do not ask USER to adopt a price or
   audience just to fill fields, and do not demand a build/actual play to author
   intent. Keep NOT_ASSESSED/false claim eligibility/false authority fixed for this
   planning artifact even when separate assessments exist.
4. Reinspect after authoring. RECONCILIATION_REQUIRED means actually read the
   changed/new/missing Idea sources, reconsider the hypotheses and unknowns, then
   deliberately update exact references. Never silently reset an authored intent
   or rehash old reviews/rulings. Bind used intent as an explicit exact input to
   any material design, not an authority path or experience evidence.
5. Report the useful hypothesis/frontier and next development experiment, and
   continue already authorized learning/production. File creation alone is not
   value analysis. A meaningful build alone introduces no whole-product report,
   independent-assessment-review or claim gate. No fixed staged-call protocol.

Idea retains post-frontier commission; Studio retains activation/archive and
baseline admission. An intent cannot serve as an assessment result, prove an
unbuilt game's value or change an adopted promise. Real material decisions still
follow their existing authority/review boundary.

## Assess actual whole-product value

At the existing whole-product stopping/price-value boundaries, or for a requested
assessment, follow the experience-based workflow. Before a paid-value claim use
the existing exact-build current/adopted-offer gate. A failed/inapplicable gate
never blocks authorized scenario learning or production.

Use the mandatory separately called protocol in product/docs/JUDGMENT_PIPELINE.md.
Frame, evidence, independent first-pass findings and decision are separate sealed
work products. Use assessment v2; the final author cannot downgrade upstream
unknowns or replace the original condition. The two independent reviewers form
condition-level judgments before seeing the proposed verdict, then compare it.
Decision conditionality is a list of strings: copy the condition values from
frame.external_conditions in order, retaining source/basis objects in the frame.
Per-condition assumptions likewise contain only those strings, never the objects.
A failed draft may receive format-only feedback in its own context; preserve the
original and record the exact correction without changing semantic findings.

Operate before recording:
1. Reconstruct the actual/current or explicitly adopted offer and intended buyer.
   Refresh material external facts; preserve source dates. A genuine proposed
   price remains a proposal, and the released price is not redesign adoption.
   Use an explicit reasonable benchmark scenario when it can answer usefully;
   don't require a user price choice merely to finish the schema. Before a decisive
   observation/repair, state the provisional buying reason, what experience would
   be enough at this offer, and what would defeat it, in the sealed frame artifact.
2. Encounter the whole relevant artifact and the experience carrying its promise.
   Challenge the strongest untested claim and flattering evidence selection.
   Art/audio/comfort/surrounding affordances matter through the coherent purchase.
3. Judge the whole offer against that contextual adequacy case. Distinguish whether
   the product is sufficient from whether evidence is sufficient to judge it.
   When serious competing explanations could change the verdict, hold build,
   buyer and offer fixed; distinguish evidence both explain from a concrete
   state/choice/consequence or observation that separates them. Resolve disputed
   factual causal claims, then make the buyer-value tradeoff. Removing an objection
   is not itself positive purchase support. Use the workflow's causal comparison;
   do not manufacture a debate or additional observation for a decisive case.
   Make a bounded subjective recommendation/rejection when justified; only an
   unknown that could change it warrants further evidence. Explain confidence and
   non-blocking limits. Explicit new user/audience facts can revise the frame;
   don't silently move goalposts or expand the proof list after the case is met.
   Explain consequential sacrifices by protected benefit
   and buyer cost. Identify the weakest causal link and the smallest useful test,
   repair, removal, reconsideration or justified stopping point. No scores, hours
   formula, content quota or default feature expansion.
4. Write the complete human-readable report plus a small exact evidence index.
   Unknown context/insufficient experience are valid conclusions. Use prepare;
   return to the two independent first-pass reviewers for exact whole-assessment
   comparison without peer conclusions.
   Preserve REVISE reports and review the exact new version after revision.
5. Record/check the result, and use the exact-build gate before a paid-value claim.
   Report the useful judgment and scope, not only “record valid.” Send bounded
   recommendations to the appropriate owner; after an authorized repair verify
   the buyer's actual experience improved. Don't review every minor edit.

Product cannot change Idea commission, price/audience, Studio lifecycle, game
content, human playtest verdict or baseline admission. Material changes require
their normal authority. Shared task completion is optional assessment tracking,
not a substitute for the mandatory real USER boundary on material designs.
