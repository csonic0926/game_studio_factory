---
name: product-factory
description: Judge whether the experienced whole game earns its actual price for its intended buyer. Challenge done/polished claims, seek decisive contrary experience, prioritize coherent value improvements and preserve honest unknowns. Not a feature score or release checklist.
---

Resolve the explicit game and selected Factory source; never scan sibling games.
Read product/AGENTS.md and product/docs/VALUE_ASSESSMENT_WORKFLOW.md. Run
product/product.py inspect --game-repo <GAME>. On explicitly versioned work, also
use factory.py inspect/context --capability product --task assessment with the
selected checkout. A legacy standalone assessment needs no migration or relink.

Operate before recording:
1. Reconstruct the actual/current or explicitly adopted offer and intended buyer.
   Refresh material external facts; preserve source dates. A genuine proposed
   price remains a proposal, and the released price is not redesign adoption.
   Use an explicit reasonable benchmark scenario when it can answer usefully;
   don't require a user price choice merely to finish the schema. Before a decisive
   observation/repair, state the provisional buying reason, what experience would
   be enough at this offer, and what would defeat it, in ordinary report prose.
2. Encounter the whole relevant artifact and the experience carrying its promise.
   Challenge the strongest untested claim and flattering evidence selection.
   Art/audio/comfort/surrounding affordances matter through the coherent purchase.
3. Judge the whole offer against that contextual adequacy case. Distinguish whether
   the product is sufficient from whether evidence is sufficient to judge it.
   Make a bounded subjective recommendation/rejection when justified; only an
   unknown that could change it warrants further evidence. Explain confidence and
   non-blocking limits. Explicit new user/audience facts can revise the frame;
   don't silently move goalposts or expand the proof list after the case is met.
   Explain consequential sacrifices by protected benefit
   and buyer cost. Identify the weakest causal link and the smallest useful test,
   repair, removal, reconsideration or justified stopping point. No scores, hours
   formula, content quota or default feature expansion.
4. Write the complete human-readable report plus a small exact evidence index.
   Unknown context/insufficient experience are valid conclusions. Use prepare;
   get two fresh independent whole-assessment reviews without peer conclusions.
   Preserve REVISE reports and review the exact new version after revision.
5. Record/check the result, and use the exact-build gate before a paid-value claim.
   Report the useful judgment and scope, not only “record valid.” Send bounded
   recommendations to the appropriate owner; after an authorized repair verify
   the buyer's actual experience improved. Don't review every minor edit.

Product cannot change Idea commission, price/audience, Studio lifecycle, game
content, human playtest verdict or baseline admission. Material changes require
their normal authority. Shared task completion is optional assessment tracking,
not a substitute for the mandatory real USER boundary on material designs.
