# Product Factory

Does the experienced whole game give its intended buyer a convincing reason to
pay its actual price? Product is the peer value specialist, complementary to
Idea's commissioned direction and Studio's playable-baseline lifecycle.

Start at [the caller landing](docs/AI_CALLER_LANDING.md) or the
[product-factory skill](skills/product-factory/SKILL.md). The
[operating workflow](docs/VALUE_ASSESSMENT_WORKFLOW.md) owns qualitative judgment.
`product.py` validates provenance, independent-review bindings and exact claim
scope; it does not calculate value. There are no points, feature quotas,
minimum hours, mandatory replay loops or automatic commercial PASSes.
