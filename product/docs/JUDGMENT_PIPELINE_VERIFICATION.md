# Separated Product judgment: bounded verification

2026-10-09. Operative code: `335a764`; original repair base: `01a0ae2`.
This is a Factory workflow repair and a bounded semantic exercise, not a new
assessment of the current SAB build, proof of willingness to pay, or USER acceptance.

## What is operative

New v2 assessments require separately produced frame, evidence and decision
artifacts. Two independent reviewers first judge the unchanged question against
raw sources, before reading the decision or one another. Their later comparisons
retain those first findings. The case preserves literal original conditions;
compound obligations and necessary factual premises remain explicit dependencies.
The final author cannot silently replace the buyer/offer, weaken a condition,
add new evidence, promote an unresolved premise or invent a taste qualification.
A circular “if this buyer finds it worthwhile” condition is not a sufficiency
answer. Historical v1 artifacts remain readable but cannot pass the repaired
current claim gate. Early intent and ordinary authorized production are unchanged.

The checker enforces identities, input closure, declared call separation and
state propagation. It does not calculate quality. New results explicitly retain
`semantic_success_proven: false`; neither formal validity nor reviewer agreement
is presented as a proof of taste or commercial success.

## Actual semantic exercise

Three fixed-source cases used actual separate contexts for frame, evidence,
decision and both independent reviewers, followed by comparison in each original
reviewer context. The positive and negative finite-product fixtures use explicitly
synthetic observer records. The historical case uses the unchanged recorded
experience and full original provisional adequacy paragraph from the earlier
price comparison. No new game play, source-game edit or current-product conclusion
was introduced.

- Finite reflective-puzzle fixture: SUPPORTED, two independent final AGREE reviews
- Finite puzzle with genuine opening pleasures but unrewarding compulsory work:
  VALUE_GAP, two independent final AGREE reviews
- Fixed historical tactical-game record: bounded scenario SUPPORTED, with the
  full original condition retained, explicit not-mostly examination, connected
  plan-development benefit and the full waiting/closure/restart cost charged

All three recorded and checked successfully; all current-offer claim gates were
blocked by their explicit scenario/hypothetical context. A positive historical
result is not a new claim about the current SAB product. Its recurrence argument
supports an explicit evaluative bridge, not a claim that shared facts uniquely
eliminate every possible negative weighting.

After freezing their actual reviews, the two historical-case reviewers separately
examined three hand-authored candidates with the full original condition copied
unchanged. Both rejected each candidate with specific source-grounded reasons:

- P1: “mostly” became “not wholly,” and local pleasures substituted for sufficiency
- P2: “if this buyer finds it worthwhile” circularly assumed the unresolved answer,
  even though the candidate said that condition was declared during framing
- P3: lack of demonstrated failure plus nonzero benefits did not establish the
  affirmative whole-purchase case

These are actual semantic comparisons, separate from schema tests. They preserve
real favorable evidence and do not require a forced negative product verdict.

## Failures retained and corrected

The first historical-case frame rejected an incomplete condition inventory and a
paraphrased question. The case owner restored the exact question and full original
adequacy paragraph, preserving raw observations and context, then called a fresh
frame. This was an actual upstream reopening, not a final-report rewrite.

Two independent finite-positive findings exposed an overstrict checker rule:
MET under an already stipulated scenario was rejected when the same external
scenario qualifications were repeated. Independent review confirmed the correction.
The untouched findings were revalidated; new/unframed preferences, unresolved
premises and missing global qualifications remain rejected.

Some outputs needed representation-only retries: projecting copied frame-condition
objects to their strings, removing redundant references already bound elsewhere,
and relocating extra narrative into allowed fields. Original outputs and failures
were retained. The same authors made those corrections; exact comparisons verified
that semantic findings, caveats, dispositions and source facts did not change.
One final comparison-label correction occurred after the separate candidate
exercise; an exact one-field diff preserves the substantive review frozen before
that exposure. Caller guidance now makes representation requirements explicit.

Independent source/runtime and final semantic review accepted this bounded repair
with no remaining release blocker. That acceptance applies to the exercised
mechanisms and arguments, not to universal judgment accuracy.

## Limits and reproducibility

The ordinary protocol has five distinct contexts and seven turns per assessment;
this exercise also incurred preserved failed attempts and format repairs. No
latency, token-saving or superiority claim follows. Actual provider token usage
was unavailable. Context separation was operator-enforced on a shared filesystem,
not a security sandbox or host-authenticated assertion of inaccessible files.

The exercise establishes these bounded behaviors and counterexample responses.
It does not establish universal semantic reliability, unique objective taste,
market demand or the quality of a current game. A reasoned finite-product judgment
may be positive or negative; replay, hours, feature totals and human purchase
studies are not universal requirements. Unresolved inference remains a legitimate
outcome rather than an invitation to invent a new observation request.

Raw packets, outputs, failure records and reviews were kept outside the Factory
source tree under one evidence boundary. Only authored code, schemas, tests,
operator guidance and this compact handoff are committed. No consumer was migrated
or relinked, and the running E&U checkout was not changed by this repair.
