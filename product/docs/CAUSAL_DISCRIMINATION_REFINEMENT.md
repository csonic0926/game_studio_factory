# Causal discrimination refinement — 2026-10-09

The earlier adequacy/stopping guidance remains necessary: define the contextual
buying reason, make bounded judgments, retain material unknowns, and stop moving
goalposts. It was not enough to ask for "convincing value" and "strongest
counterevidence." Opposite conclusions can redescribe the same observations while
leaving their actual causal disagreement unresolved. Refuting one weak rejection
is not affirmative evidence that a purchase is worthwhile.

The current workflow adds an informative intermediate operation when ambiguity
is material: hold build/buyer/offer fixed, formulate serious causal explanations,
identify shared versus discriminating observations, resolve concrete factual
claims, and then state the remaining buyer-value tradeoff. It preserves observer
self-reports as scoped evidence. It neither treats subjective true price as known
nor forces different verdicts at different prices.

This is a bounded guidance/template correction. It adds no runtime schema, score,
feature quota, extra reviewer role or mandatory two-price ritual. It does not
change Idea commission, Gameplay analysis, Studio acceptance, offer authority or
historical receipts. The workflow is already a sealed Factory dependency, so its
changed bytes invalidate reuse of old assessment reviews rather than refreshing
them into new approval.

The old semantic scenarios often stipulate that an experience is satisfying or
empty; they primarily test boundary behavior. The new ambiguous packet instead
supplies state/choice/consequence traces, observation limits and model-observer
statements. Its separate coordinator protocol checks factual discrimination and
reasoning without a predetermined purchase label. It still cannot establish
human willingness to pay or population accuracy. Independent execution/review of
that packet must be reported separately from engineering test results.
