# Whole-product judgment repair: bounded design

2026-10-09. Scope: new Product assessments in the selected Factory checkout.
This changes assessment operation and admission; it does not commission a game,
change consumers, add a production stop, or retroactively upgrade old assessments.
Raw execution evidence lives outside this repository. The earlier six-call
experiment remains historical and is not activated.

## Failure being repaired

An author can currently set the adequacy bar, interpret experience, reduce a
whole-purchase objection to an accepted limit and announce SUPPORTED. Reviewers
see the completed answer before judging the original question. Correct citations
and defensible personal weighting then substitute for the stronger original
condition. In the audited example, “mostly administrative / too unrewarding as a
whole” becomes “not wholly administrative.”

Idea contributes useful units, not a magic detector or an existing isolated-call
pipeline: outcome → player reason → experienced mechanism → sacrifice/cost →
uncertain belief and falsifier. Product must retain those distinct propositions
and their scope across actual calls.

## Operative boundary

New assessments use an explicit v2 contract and separate fresh contexts for:

1. **Frame**: receive the exact task/authority, buyer/offer/build and raw source
   packet, without any completed worth-it answer. Preserve the original question
   and literal conditions; decompose its whole-experience causal obligations.
   Explain each condition's meaning, scope, benefit/cost and what would defeat it.
   Identify source fact, authority, hypothesis and evaluator criterion separately.
   This is retrospective if experience was already seen, never preregistration.
2. **Evidence**: receive the case, raw sources and frozen frame. Resolve each
   condition's experiential premises against exact source locations. Preserve
   positive and contrary evidence, entity/build/time/population limits, shared
   observations, discriminators and material unknowns. An evidence-sufficiency
   judgment is different from whether a proposition is true. No purchase verdict.
3. **Decision**: receive only the case, frame, evidence and complete raw closure.
   Resolve every original condition, give the integrated benefit/cost reason for
   the same buyer/occasion/offer, and show why the strongest actual objection is
   met or defeats the purchase. The decision author cannot alter source states,
   weaken a condition, or reclassify an unresolved premise as an accepted cost.
   A new premise or framing change reopens its owning stage and invalidates the
   dependent outputs. A qualified subjective judgment is permitted; an essential
   ungrounded preference stays an explicit truth condition of the recommendation.
4. **Two independent reviewers, each in two passes**: first receive raw sources,
   case and frame only, and seal their own condition-level findings before seeing
   evidence-stage interpretation, decision or final report. Then compare the
   completed assessment with that preserved independent view. Each receives its
   own first pass, never its peer's. Changing its finding requires a specific
   reason grounded in the same question, not author/reviewer agreement.

These are five distinct contexts and seven turns in the ordinary path, rather
than six copies of a report. No fixed counts of conditions, features, observations,
comparisons, hours or repeat runs are imposed. Only actual distinct calls count;
one author filling all headings is inadmissible. The CLI produces bounded packets
and checks declared context identity, dependency closure and order. This is
operator-enforced context isolation on a shared filesystem, not host attestation
or a claim that arbitrary outside reads are impossible.

## Small work products and enforced dependencies

A game-owned case file fixes the question, exact original condition text and
source locations, context and complete evidence refs. The CLI's `packet` command
returns the role prompt, required output shape and the exact allowed input refs.
Stage outputs bind that packet digest. Frame/evidence/decision artifacts are
separate, game-owned, exact-hash inputs to assessment v2. Each downstream artifact
repeats condition IDs and literal original text; it cannot drop or rename one.
A condition with an unresolved material experiential premise cannot become MET.
Factual findings distinguish supported, contradicted and unresolved. Decision
findings distinguish MET, NOT_MET, UNRESOLVED and CONDITIONAL, without pretending
these enum labels prove meaning. Essential conditional premises propagate to the
assessment's conditionality and block an unconditional current-offer claim.

Every condition gets an explicit disposition and dependency explanation, including
how local benefits compose into the whole experience and the full strongest cost.
SUPPORTED requires all conditions MET or explicitly CONDITIONAL, no unresolved
material premise and a whole-purchase synthesis beyond local pleasures. A genuine
finite arc can pass or fail without replay. VALUE_GAP remains valid completed work.
EVIDENCE_INSUFFICIENT names an actual decision-changing missing observation;
JUDGMENT_UNRESOLVED identifies an unresolved inference/criterion and calls for
argument repair, not invented play or population proof. CONTEXT_UNRESOLVED remains
available. No universal positive or negative outcome is programmed.

First-pass reviewer records bind only their blind packet and own context. Final
v2 reviews require the same context, an exact reference to that first pass, and
condition-by-condition semantic comparison. No final review can silently erase an
unresolved independent finding. It may justify a changed judgment using existing
source/reasoning; actual new facts or changed conditions require upstream reopening.
The normal result remains a mechanical receipt, explicitly not semantic proof.
A v1 assessment remains readable under historical compatibility but cannot create
a new current-pipeline receipt or be described as repaired validation. New prepare
and record require v2; old records are never silently migrated or rehashed.

## Reopening and admission

Immutable stage records bind their inputs. Revisions use a new assessment/run ID
and retain old outputs; reuse is allowed only for unaffected exact inputs. A stale
source/frame invalidates descendants; an unresolved issue survives every consumer.
A stage can request reopening its earliest affected predecessor and stop dependent
work. Operator must perform the fresh upstream call and regenerate descendants;
quarantining an error while claiming the entire chain passes is forbidden.
Schema/IDs check bookkeeping only. Reviewers check actual meaning, quantifiers,
causal sufficiency and sampling. Neither record validity nor reviewer agreement is
reported as proof of product quality or proven workflow effectiveness.

  binding, legacy refusal for new admission, negative and finite positive cases
- Actual separated calls on neutral finite-product fixtures, preserving raw first

  the original mostly/wholly weakening, and “not proven bad therefore enough”
- Independent architecture/final reviewer checks both code and semantic outputs,
