# Adequacy and stopping refinement — 2026-10-09

The initial Product Factory correctly separated source validation from judgment
and allowed honest uncertainty. Its first real exercise exposed a narrower
operating gap: "representative experience" and "stop when the promise is met"
did not require a concrete account of what would actually earn the stated price.
A reviewer could keep listing unmeasured concerns without ever recommending or
rejecting the whole product. That is not sufficient commercial judgment.

This is a small guidance/template refinement, not a new score, schema, ledger,
review role or evidence quota. Before a decisive observation or further repair,
the report states a provisional buying/adequacy case grounded in the offer,
buyer, occasion and credible alternatives, plus observed support/falsification
anchors. It distinguishes product sufficiency from sufficiency of evidence to
judge it. Ordinary confidence limits remain distinct from decision-changing
material unknowns and actual conditional truth claims.

The model must make a reasoned bounded recommendation or rejection when evidence
supports it; uncertainty alone does not justify indefinite deferral. A genuine
remaining decision-changing gap may still block a verdict, with a specific
bounded next observation or actual access blocker. New material user/audience
facts can legitimately overturn a provisional frame, with an explicit reason
and preserved history. The original frame is not user authority, and a weak
self-authored promise is not its own proof of price-value adequacy.

Conditional scenario judgment remains useful without adopting its price. The
separate actual-offer gate must not be confused with whether the scenario question
has received a substantive answer. Old assessed artifacts and reviews remain
unchanged; new contract content correctly changes the dependency fingerprint for
new/current assessments, rather than rehashing historical reviews into approval.

Three added qualitative scenarios challenge endless proof requests after adequate
experience, evidence-rich but weak value, and arbitrary goalpost shifts versus
legitimate reframing. They are fictional semantic exercises, not runtime evidence
or mechanically provable claims about taste. Any uncoached live observer remains
isolated from these informed criteria; evaluate its sealed observations afterward.
