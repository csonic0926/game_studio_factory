# Product Factory: judge the coherent purchase

## Start before there is a build

Game initialization establishes both Idea work and a separate nonbinding
Product value intent through `setup.py init`. Read
[PROJECT_INITIALIZATION_WORKFLOW.md](PROJECT_INITIALIZATION_WORKFLOW.md).
Author the provisional whole buying reason, contextual adequacy case, hypotheses
and smallest useful next observation from current sources and delegated scope.
An empty seed is not that analysis. Unknowns and scenario prices are legitimate;
no build, invented play, current-offer PASS or new price-adoption gate is required
for early learning. Intent cannot replace Idea commission or later assessment.

## Required call ownership for new assessments

Follow [JUDGMENT_PIPELINE.md](JUDGMENT_PIPELINE.md). Its separate frame, evidence,
decision and two pre-verdict independent-review contexts are operative, not optional
headings. Use judgment-packet/judgment-seal and assessment/review v2. The original
question and every compound condition survive the chain. Evidence, source scope,
assumptions and verdict remain distinct. New premises or changed conditions reopen
upstream work; an unresolved whole-purchase inference cannot become an accepted
limit inside the final author. Historical v1 work is not silently upgraded.

## The job

When a producer says “done,” “polished enough,” or “worth its price,” reconstruct
what is being sold to whom and encounter the experience that carries that claim.
Find the most decisive reason the buyer could reasonably feel disappointed.
Then judge the whole proposition and choose the smallest useful next move. Do
this again at a meaningful changed-product boundary to establish whether the
experience actually improved. Stop expanding when the coherent promise is met.
A receipt at the end supports this work; generating receipts is not the work.

A premise can be interesting yet the purchased product disappointing. Conversely,
a short authored experience can justify a substantial price, and a small cheap
game can deliver a complete worthwhile purchase. A score cannot settle this.
Replay, art, audio, story, tactile response, interface, comfort and surrounding
features matter through their contribution to this buyer's experience. No one
factor can automatically compensate for an unfulfilled central promise.

## Reconstruct the offer without rewriting it

Resolve only the explicit game root and selected Factory source. Read existing
product authority and the game's own requirements. Record:

- Actual offer: exact amount/currency, platform, region and observation date
- Intended buyer and the reason this experience might matter to them
- What the promise commits the whole product to, including deliberate limits
- Which context is fact/adoption, a proposal, an AI hypothesis or still unknown

CURRENT_OFFER means an observed actual offer. ADOPTED_OFFER requires evidence of
explicit adoption in current user/product authority. Preserve its exact quote
and game-owned raw USER source in offer.adoption and provenance; reviewers must
judge its actual adoption meaning, not merely find the quoted words. A user saying “what about
$5?” is PROPOSED_OFFER, even when that quote is genuine. SCENARIO is conditional
analysis. Neither a proposal nor a scenario becomes the actual offer merely
because the value case at that price is attractive. Keep unknown actual context
explicit. When useful, evaluate a clearly declared, evidence-grounded benchmark
scenario instead of stopping to make the user populate a schema. Ask only when a
necessary choice belongs to the user. A released game's price is not automatically
the redesigned game's selected price, but it can be a useful conditional benchmark.

Refresh material storefront/market facts from their authoritative sources before
making a new current-offer claim. Save URLs, observation dates and relevant facts
in a game-owned source artifact. A checked hash certifies the unchanged capture,
not live external freshness. The ordinary-language verdict retains this limit.

Absence of Factory commission files does not require commissioning a historical
released game just to assess it. Distinguish absent authority from partial/broken
canonical files and from an explicit NO_ACTIVE_PRODUCT_AUTHORITY register. An
archived product can be studied historically; it cannot pass as currently active.
Idea owns commission/reconsideration, and studio/product.py owns its lifecycle.

## State what would be enough, then make the judgment

Before the decisive observation or further repair, make a provisional adequacy
case visible in the separately sealed frame before the decision call. A short paragraph can be enough:
why this buyer might choose/pay for this whole experience, what experienced
benefit would justify this offer despite its limits, and what contrary experience
would defeat that recommendation. Ground the bar in the actual offer, buyer,
occasion and plausible alternatives, including not buying. A weak self-authored
promise does not justify a price merely because the software fulfills it.

Alternatives supply context for the buying reason, not a competitor-count quota,
feature-copy list or demand to beat every nearby game. Research a named comparison
when a factual claim about it matters; distinguish its advertised offer from
firsthand comparative experience. When context is uncertain, label the provisional
buyer/offer instead of inventing adoption or refusing useful conditional judgment.

Keep two questions separate:

- Product sufficiency: does the experienced whole give this buyer a worthwhile
  reason to choose it at this price, with these deliberate sacrifices? Modest art,
  short duration or limited replay can fit a convincing whole. A good mechanism
  or low price does not excuse an experience the buyer must imagine becoming good
- Evidence sufficiency: have the value-bearing experience and strongest plausible
  counterexample been encountered enough to make that bounded judgment? This is
  not exhaustive verification, statistical proof of demand or elimination of all
  uncertainty. Enough evidence can support either a recommendation or a rejection

For this actual product, say what observed experience would support the value
case and what would falsify it before requesting the next decisive test. Do not
turn these anchors into universal pass boxes. If evidence already permits a
judgment, make it now rather than inventing a study to satisfy this instruction.
A serious observed failure of the central buying reason can establish VALUE_GAP
without first investigating every other aspect of the game.

Call an unknown material only when a plausible answer could change the purchase
recommendation, and explain how. Record ordinary residual limits and confidence
in the report/evidence limitations. Do not put "no population study" or an unknown
unpromised long-term retention rate into material_unknowns merely because they
are unmeasured. conditionality is for actual truth conditions (for example a
proposed offer), not a synonym for moderate confidence. A qualified model judgment
is useful; it is neither fabricated human taste nor a forecast of market success.

After the discriminating experience, return to the stated adequacy case. Decide
SUPPORTED or VALUE_GAP when justified, explaining the whole-product reason,
confidence and remaining non-blocking limits. Stop adding scope/proof requests
when that case is met. If genuinely unable to decide, EVIDENCE_INSUFFICIENT must
name the missing decision-changing observation, the plausible opposing outcomes,
and the smallest way to distinguish them (or the exact access blocker). Do not
force a binary verdict when that material gap is real, and do not defer forever
by appending an unspecific list of pacing/audio/replay concerns.

Actual user/audience evidence or a newly discovered material fact may overturn
the provisional frame. Explain the new fact and why it changes the bar, preserve
the earlier reasoning, and make the revision explicit before further evaluation.
Do not silently move goalposts because the original test passed, narrow the
commissioned promise to excuse a failure, or mistake the assessor's initial frame
for user authority. Material direction changes still belong to Idea/USER.

## Encounter the value-bearing experience

Play or inspect the exact runnable build/manifest and the material visual/audio
output. Observe what the buyer does, feels, understands, carries forward and
returns for, or what makes the conclusion satisfying. Actual evidence can include
play traces, recordings, inspected media and contextualized player observations.
Describe what each source supports and what it does not. Bind it to the exact
build; keep technical TEST evidence separate from EXPERIENCE observations.

Follow the promise, not a fixed playtime quota:

- If the purchase rests on mastery/replay, an excellent opening doesn't establish
  later adaptation, meaningful repetition or a durable desire to return
- If it rests on a short authored experience, encounter the arc and ending; do
  not impose replay or inflate its hours to imitate a different kind of game
- If atmosphere or expression carries the offer, inspect the actual art, motion,
  sound and composition in use. Asset cost/resolution and correct layout prove
  neither aesthetic intent nor its emotional effect
- If a surrounding affordance matters to the play situation, try that situation.
  Saving, restart, input comfort or discovery can support or undermine the main
  experience. They do not become universal requirements for unrelated games

Actively look for decisive contrary experience. What is the strongest material
claim not reached by the footage/session? Was the sample selected because it is
flattering? Could a coherent whole be present despite modest individual parts?
Name unavailable central experience rather than filling it with plausible prose.
A nonempty runtime ref is only a floor; sufficiency is qualitative judgment.

## Make the value case, including difficult choices

### Resolve competing explanations when they matter

When the same experience plausibly supports opposing purchase recommendations,
or a material factual claim is disputed, do this causal comparison before choosing
the verdict. Keep the exact build, buyer, offer and observed scope fixed. Do not
manufacture an opposing case for an already decisive, uncomplicated observation.

1. State the strongest serious explanations of the experience, not merely
   "worth it" versus "not worth it." Connect what the player does and what changes
   to a benefit or cost this buyer has reason to value. Each explanation must fit
   the strongest inconvenient observations; do not defeat a weaker substitute.
2. Identify the observations both explanations can account for. Those observations
   are not discriminators just because one account retells them persuasively.
   Then identify the concrete claim on which the explanations differ and the
   observed state/choice/consequence, inspected media or contextual fact that
   supports one over the other. Cite the exact source location. State what each
   explanation would lead you to expect there. If the existing evidence settles
   the claim, resolve it now; do not request another report or playthrough by habit.
3. Separate factual disagreement from buyer-value weighting. Whether a choice
   changes later options is a factual causal claim; whether that kind of control
   outweighs repetition or modest presentation at this offer is a reasoned value
   tradeoff. Resolve the former against evidence. For the latter, state the
   protected benefit, buyer cost and why that balance warrants the recommendation
   for this same buyer. Do not disguise a taste disagreement as a missing fact or
   silently recruit a different audience. Model-observer reactions are evidence
   of that observer's experience, not population preference or objective quality.
4. Return to the provisional adequacy case. Explain what positive purchase case
   survives the strongest objection, or what observed shortcoming defeats it.
   Refuting a weak negative rationale only removes that objection; it is not
   affirmative support for buying. Conversely, a working or unusual mechanism
   establishes neither worthwhile interaction nor whole-product value by itself.
   If an unresolved factual distinction could reverse the judgment, give its
   opposing outcomes and the smallest discriminating observation. Otherwise make
   the bounded tradeoff and stop, retaining confidence and non-blocking limits.

Make this intermediate reasoning inspectable in ordinary prose at the depth the
dispute needs. Labels such as "meaningful," "polished," "adequate" or "strongest
counterevidence" do not perform it. Neither reviewer agreement nor more fluent
argument can replace the missing causal link. This is not a new scorecard, fixed
number of alternatives, debate ritual, feature quota or requirement for paired
price reports. A changed price may alter the weight of the same delivered benefit;
it cannot change what happened in the build. Retain negative experience as well as
positive evidence, and preserve genuine decision-changing unknowns.

Write PRODUCT_VALUE_REPORT.md as one coherent reasoned account, at the depth the
actual question needs. Explain the purchase promise, delivered experience,
price/audience relationship, causal fit among parts, strongest counterevidence,
material unknowns and the next move. It need not be a fixed rubric or long report.
Relevant tradeoffs explain the protected benefit AND the buyer's cost. Listing
absent features is not a sacrifice argument. Don't invent a sacrifice if none is
material. Comparisons are optional discriminators with comparability limits,
never a feature-extraction quota or hours-per-dollar formula.

Find the weakest causal link in the purchase case. Examples of useful decisions:
refine a confusing interaction that prevents the central pleasure; integrate art
and sound around the existing action; remove a mode that dilutes the promise;
observe the untested endgame before prescribing fixes; reconsider an offer with
the user; or stop adding work because the focused experience already earns it.
A recommendation names its owner and what evidence would change the conclusion.
Don't spend/produce/change the product merely to make this assessment pass.

Judgments:

- SUPPORTED: the stated contextual adequacy case is met by sufficient experience;
  no unresolved decision-changing gap. Explain bounded confidence and residual
  limits. A proposed/scenario recommendation remains conditional
- VALUE_GAP: observed shortcomings undermine the whole offer; identify why and
  prioritize the meaningful next choice, rather than add features by default
- EVIDENCE_INSUFFICIENT: a named missing observation could change the recommendation;
  state the decision split and bounded next test or actual blocker
- JUDGMENT_UNRESOLVED: the decisive whole-purchase inference remains unsupported;
  repair the argument or frame without inventing a missing observation
- CONTEXT_UNRESOLVED: target price or intended audience remains unknown

All five can be useful completed assessments. No outcome grants USER acceptance,
changes a thesis, promises market success or replaces gameplay/baseline admission.

## Independent boundary judgment

First seal each reviewer's independent condition findings from raw evidence and frame,
without a proposed verdict, under JUDGMENT_PIPELINE.md. Then prepare the exact package
with product.py prepare. Return the same assessment,
report and full relevant evidence closure to the two original independent contexts using
product-value-reviewer: intent_experience and completeness_project. They are
informed reviewers and cannot be described as blind players. Any blind runtime
observation remains a separately sanitized existing Studio artifact. Each sees
all sources and assesses the whole case. Neither sees first-pass peer conclusions.

Both reviewers must challenge evidence selection, the strongest untested
material claim, source sufficiency, causal value and consequential omissions.
They must also judge whether the provisional adequacy bar is fair to the buyer,
whether its support/falsification anchors were actually addressed, and whether a
remaining unknown could change the recommendation. Reject both unsupported praise
and avoidant deferral. For a material competing explanation, check the actual
disputed causal claim against its cited observation: does the alleged discriminator
really distinguish the accounts, or do both predict it? Separate any remaining
value-weight disagreement from factual error. A rebutted objection alone cannot
support AGREE with a positive purchase verdict. If the case is met, do not demand unrelated features, larger
studies or new standards; accept a justified bounded judgment. Legitimate reframing
needs an explicit new material reason, not obedience to a permanently fixed bar.
The roles give complementary emphasis, not permission to ignore half the product.
AGREE means “this assessment is sound,” including a justified gap or uncertainty;
REVISE means repair the argument/evidence/conclusion. Never average disagreement
into support. Keep failed reports and use a new assessment/version after material
revision. Only actual new fresh reviews of that exact package may pass.

The tool checks declared fresh/distinct IDs and source hashes; the harness and
operator are responsible for actual context isolation and honest observation.
Nonempty prose, SHA-256 references and reviewer labels cannot prove good taste,
truth, sufficient experience, fun or willingness to pay. Inspect the work.

## Handoff and return to production

Publish the immutable result with record and check it. Before a worth-buying
claim, Studio calls studio/product.py check-value with the actual exact build.
The gate only supports grounded CURRENT_OFFER/ADOPTED_OFFER judgments without
material conditionality. It returns exact price/currency/platform/region/date,
audience and provenance, including on a blocked result. Proposed/scenario support
cannot be shortened to an unconditional current-offer claim. A conditional
recommendation can still complete the substantive price-value question; refusal
by this narrower current-offer gate is not a failed scenario judgment or a demand
for user price reapproval. New build/context/evidence or
relevant authority requires reassessment, not rewriting old receipt hashes.

Tell the user the scoped judgment, decisive evidence/limit and useful next move.
Studio can use the assessment to prioritize already authorized work. Material
promise, audience, price or scope changes go to the user/Idea; bounded execution
goes to Gameplay, Story, Asset or Sound. An accepted playable slice can continue
learning/production without a whole-product value PASS. Do not block prototypes,
retroactively rewrite accepted baselines, or mint USER verdicts.

## Shared v2/v3 tasks and historical games

Standalone CLI assessment is additive and requires no migration or user approval
of a study/negative finding. On an explicitly versioned project, Product context
is available through factory.py; open work can remain DRAFT. Material designs
follow the selected shared workflow, two complete-design reviews and its real
USER ruling boundary. Never invent that ruling from the assessment commission.

A Product design's `product` binding seals assessment id, subject, exact offer
and audience, result path and required outputs inside production_scope. The
Product acceptance adapter requires all promised current outputs in the actual
preceding EVIDENCE_READY checkpoint and in COMPLETE, then matches every bound
identity/context field. Another valid assessment cannot close this task, nor can
late COMPLETE-only outputs. COMPLETE means assessment finished even when its
judgment is negative; it does not mean the product passed the claim gate.
